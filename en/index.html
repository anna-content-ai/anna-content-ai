About me

Anna ZiółkowskaContent Marketing & AI Specialist · Warsaw

For over 9 years I’ve worked in content marketing and brand communication for B2B and B2C: from content strategy, through campaigns and email marketing, to trade fairs and events. Today I combine this with practical use of AI.

At a building materials manufacturer I’m responsible for content strategy and social media copy (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn), campaigns and contests, industry events and working with the agency. I also roll out AI tools in the team and run AI workshops.

I completed the postgraduate programme “Artificial Intelligence and Automation in Marketing” at SGH Warsaw School of Economics, with a thesis on fake promotions impersonating airline brands. I hold Google Analytics (2026) and HubSpot Email Marketing certifications. I build and test AI tools myself, from prompts to Claude Skills on GitHub.
