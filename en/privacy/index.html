Privacy and cookie policy

This site uses no tracking cookies, analytics or ads. Your data reaches me only if you write to me yourself.

In short

No cookies, no Google Analytics, no ad pixels. Your browser only remembers that the privacy notice was closed.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Anna Ziółkowska. Privacy contact: aiprosciej@gmail.com.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Cookies: the site doesn’t set any.
  • Browser storage (localStorage): one entry, privacy-notice, remembers that the privacy notice was closed. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can delete it in your browser settings.
  • Fonts are hosted on the same server as the site, so opening it doesn’t connect you to Google Fonts.

Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any server, GitHub may log technical visit data, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure and working. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Form and email

The form on this site doesn’t send or store anything. It opens your email app with a ready message. When you send it, I receive your name, email address, company name if given, and the message.

I use them only to reply and to have the conversation you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). I keep the correspondence as long as the conversation needs it, then delete it.

External links

Links to LinkedIn and GitHub lead to other services. Once you open them, their privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its processing and to object. Just write to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority, UODO (uodo.gov.pl).
